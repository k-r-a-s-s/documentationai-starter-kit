---
title: Can't Log In
description: What to do if you can't access your account.
---

## Symptoms

- Incorrect password error
- Forgot your email or password
- Login page not loading

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    Make sure you are using the correct email. Search for previous emails from High Impact Athletes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Sometimes clearing your cache helps resolve login page issues.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">Still stuck? Email support@highimpactathletes.com</Callout>
