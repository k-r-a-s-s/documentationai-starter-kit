---
title: Create a New Impact Project
description: Step-by-step to start your own High Impact Athletes project.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your dashboard">
    Log in and click on "New Project"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter project details">
    - Name
    - Purpose
    - Goals
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite teammates">
    Add emails of fellow athletes to your project team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click "Create Project" to get started and share your initiative.
  </Step>
</Steps>
