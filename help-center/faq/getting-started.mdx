## Getting Started

1. Go to `https://highimpactathletes.com`
2. Click the "Sign Up" button
3. Fill out your name, email, and password
4. Verify your email address

## Joining a Project

1. Browse available projects on your dashboard
2. Click "Join Project" to get involved

## Questions?
Contact support anytime at support@highimpactathletes.com
